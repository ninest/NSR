The Individual Physical Proficiency Test (IPPT) has three stations: push ups, sit ups, and a 2.4 km run. Each station is scored by age group, for a total of 100 points.

To pass you need **at least 51 points**, with **at least 1 point in every station**.

This page has a calculator, the current scoring and awards, and tips to push your score up.

## Calculator

To work out your score, use the [ippt.yctay.com calculator](https://ippt.yctay.com/). Enter your age, reps, and run timing, and it gives your points and award.

If you're exempted from any of the stations, use the official [IPPT scoring calculator on NS.SG](https://www.ns.sg/web/portal/nsmen/home/nstopics/ippt-ipt-rt/ippt/ippt-stations-and-scoring-system/scoring-calculation) instead, as it handles the adjusted scoring.

## Scores and awards

Your total is out of 100. The award bands:

Award | Points | NSmen incentive
-- | -- | --
Fail | below 51 | none
Pass | 51 to 60 | none
Pass with incentive | 61 to 74 | **$200**
Silver | 75 to 84 | **$300**
Gold | 85 to 100 | **$500**

- You must score **at least 1 point in each station** to pass, even if your total is already above 51.
- The same amounts apply to **NSFs (in their unit window), NSmen, and Regulars**.
- **No money during training.** The pre-enlistment IPPT, the [BMT](/basic-military-training) IPPT, and any IPPT you take while in SCS or OCS don't pay out. They're training tests. You only become eligible once you're **posted to your unit** after BMT (and after command school, if you go).
- You only get one award payout per IPPT window. Retake and score higher, and your incentive is topped up to the higher award.
- Some combat vocations are held to a higher gold standard. Check the official site for your exact requirement.

*Incentive amounts have been in place since 2014. Last verified: May 2026. Sources: [OneNS IPPT awards and incentives](https://www.ns.sg/web/portal/nsmen/home/nstopics/ippt-ipt-rt/ippt/ippt-stations-and-scoring-system/awards-and-incentives) and [MINDEF (Feb 2014)](https://www.mindef.gov.sg/news-and-events/latest-releases/2014feb04-Forum-Letter-056).*

## How to score gold

The run is worth the most, so that is where golds are won and lost. For a quick overview, watch [How to get gold for IPPT](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FDNIz3FVf10).

### Prioritise the 2.4 km run
- The run is **50 of the 100 points**, more than push ups and sit ups combined.
- Shaving time off the run is usually the fastest way to jump from pass to silver, or silver to gold.
- Train it 2 to 3 times a week. Mix interval runs for speed with one longer steady run for endurance.

### Push ups and sit ups
- Only **counted reps** score. Bad-form reps get waved off, so train the correct form, not just the number.
- Push ups: lock your arms out at the top, chest near the ground at the bottom.
- Sit ups: elbows must touch the knees, then shoulder blades back down each rep.
- A few sets every day builds your reps fast over a couple of weeks.
- Unsure of the counting standard? Watch [CNA's push up form demo](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uMAYtVCMzAs), or see the [official IPPT stations guide](https://www.ns.sg/web/portal/nsmen/home/nstopics/ippt-ipt-rt/ippt/ippt-stations-and-scoring-system/ippt-stations).

### Plan your timing
- Book your IPPT **early in your window**, not at the last minute. This leaves room for free retakes if you miss the award you wanted.
- Warm up properly before the run. A cold start costs you seconds.

## IPPT and NS FIT for NSmen

IPT and RT were replaced by **NS FIT** in 2021. Under the current system, an eligible NSman can fulfil the annual fitness requirement by either:

- Passing IPPT; or
- Completing **10 NS FIT sessions**, including one IPPT attempt

If you already attempted IPPT but did not pass, that attempt can count as the IPPT component of the 10-session programme. If you pass IPPT while doing NS FIT, you have fulfilled the requirement and do not need to complete the remaining sessions.

If you do not pass IPPT by the end of your window, you will be placed in a **Mandatory NS FIT window** next. You must complete the 10 mandatory sessions, unless you pass IPPT during that window and fulfil the requirement early.

Book early so illness, work, or unavailable slots near the end of the window do not leave you stuck.

## Missed everything? Fines and summary trials

A missed booking does not fulfil the annual requirement. Reschedule within your window if the booking system permits. The important point is to fulfil the applicable requirement before the window ends.

- If you did not pass IPPT in your regular window, you will be required to complete 10 sessions in the next Mandatory NS FIT window
- If you do not complete a Mandatory NS FIT window, MINDEF says you will be called back for investigation and may face disciplinary action
- If you are directed to attend a Summary Trial, follow the notice and attend. Outcomes are case-specific, so do not rely on a fixed fine amount or an unofficial penalty ladder
- A Stern Warning Letter can require you to pass IPPT or complete 10 NS FIT sessions in the current window. Follow the instructions in the letter

If illness or a medical condition affects your ability to complete the requirement, contact your unit and obtain a proper medical assessment. Do not wait until the window closes.

Official sources: [What is Mandatory NS FIT?](https://ask.gov.sg/mindef/questions/cmh4fdas8022buk1bag0u80b6), [what happens when an IPPT or NS FIT requirement is not completed](https://ask.gov.sg/mindef/questions/cmh4fdarz01whuk1br6iye9v9), and [what to do after a Stern Warning Letter](https://ask.gov.sg/mindef/questions/cmh4fdasu02fzuk1burl9ae3l).

## Exemptions and partial tests
- If you're medically excused a station (for example your [PES status](/pes) or an injury), you take a modified test, and your pass mark and scoring are adjusted.
- Use the official [IPPT scoring calculator on NS.SG](https://www.ns.sg/web/portal/nsmen/home/nstopics/ippt-ipt-rt/ippt/ippt-stations-and-scoring-system/scoring-calculation) to work out your standard if you're exempted any station.

## Common questions

**What is the IPPT window?**
For NSmen it runs birthday to birthday. For NSFs it's the test window your unit gives you.

**What happens if I fail?**
For an NSman, an unsuccessful attempt is not the same as defaulting. You can try again or complete NS FIT. If the window closes without a pass, the next window becomes a Mandatory NS FIT window. For **pre-enlistees**, you can book another attempt while you remain eligible and have enough time before enlistment.

**What happens if I ignore IPPT and NS FIT entirely (NSmen)?**
You can be treated as a defaulter, called back for investigation, required to attend a Summary Trial, and subjected to disciplinary action. See [missed everything? fines and summary trials](#missed-everything-fines-and-summary-trials).

**How many tries do I get for the pre-enlistee IPPT?**
Unlimited, as long as you still have enough time before enlistment to book another slot. Take it as many times as you can fit in.

**Why bother passing the pre-enlistee IPPT?**
If you're PES A or B1 and score **61 or more** (with at least 1 point per station), you skip the **8-week Physical Training Phase (PTP)**. That cuts about **2 months** off your full-time NS, so it's well worth clearing before you enlist.

**Do I get the incentive money during BMT or command school?**
No. The pre-enlistment IPPT, the BMT IPPT, and IPPT during SCS or OCS are training tests and don't pay out. You're eligible once you're posted to your unit.

**Can I retake for a higher award?**
Yes. Take it as many times as your window allows. Your incentive tops up to your best result.

**Which station matters most?**
The run. It's half your score.
