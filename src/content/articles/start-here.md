Not sure where to begin? This page is like the MRT map. Find the section that sounds like you, then follow the links.

You have NS coming, or you're trying to figure out what it involves.

- Understand what NS is and how it starts: [BMT guide](/basic-military-training) and [vocations](/vocations)
- Decode the slang: [army lingo and NS terms](/lingo)
- Waiting for your medical exams: [PES status](/pes), [up-PESing](/guide-on-uppes), [what Attend C means](/what-is-attend-c), [glasses requirement](/glasses-requirement), and the [FAQ](/faq)
- Packing and shopping before enlistment: [before enlistment checklist](/before-enlistment), [hair requirement](/hair-requirement), and [SAF eMart locations and army shops](/army-shop-emart)
- Learning about basic training: [BMT guide](/basic-military-training), [BMT Hub](/bmt-hub), [IPPT calculator](/ippt), and [enlistment dates](/dates)
- Studying or living overseas: [CMPB overseas pre-enlistees](https://www.cmpb.gov.sg/before-ns/overseas-pre-enlistees/)
